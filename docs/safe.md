# SAFe-lite Delivery

The template supports lightweight SAFe-inspired planning without requiring a full enterprise rollout.

## Recommended issue hierarchy

- Epic: strategic objective or large initiative.
- Feature: deliverable slice under an epic.
- Story: implementable unit of work.
- Enabler: tooling, automation, architecture, or dependency work.
- Spike: time-boxed research.
- Bug: defect or regression.
- Decision: human-in-the-loop artifact.

Use the `PI` field for planning increments and `Iteration` for team cadence.
