# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-30_

untool.ai ("**untool**", "**we**", "**us**") operates in stealth. This Privacy Policy describes
how we handle information in connection with this documentation site and related preview
materials. It is deliberately minimal because we collect deliberately little.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Site analytics.** Aggregate, non-identifying usage data (pages viewed, approximate region,
  referrer) to understand what's useful. We do not build advertising profiles.
- **Information you volunteer.** If you contact us, we receive whatever you choose to send
  (e.g., your email address and message).

We do **not** require accounts to read this site, and we do not knowingly collect information
from anyone under 16.

## 2. How we use it

To operate, secure, and improve the documentation and the platform it describes, and to respond
to you when you reach out. That's it.

## 3. Sharing

We do **not** sell personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us
run the site (e.g., hosting and analytics), under confidentiality obligations, and where required
by law.

## 4. Retention & security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and apply reasonable technical
and organizational safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

## 5. Your choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information we
hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise them, contact us below.

## 6. International

untool operates as a stealth-stage entity; processing may occur in jurisdictions other than your
own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy as the platform matures. Material changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above.

## 8. Contact

Privacy enquiries: **privacy@untool.ai**.
