# Policy Objects

> [!abstract] Pattern
> Make safety and governance a *composable object model* — not scattered if-statements.

**🎛 [[Obsidian Board]] · 🧩 [[OOP Patterns for Agentic Platforms]] · 🧪 [[Governance in the Model]]**

## The move

- Policies are modeled (versioned, reviewed, testable)
- Scenarios evaluate policies before effects
- Evidence includes policy decisions (“why was this allowed?”)

> [!tip] Why this matters
> The platform can only scale agent autonomy when policy is explicit and auditable.
